We just open-sourced a WordPress plugin that turns your podcast into a browsable carousel with a floating player.

It's called amplifi.pods.

Here's the problem with podcast embeds on WordPress:

Apple Podcasts gives you one episode at a time.
Spotify gives you one episode at a time.
There's no native way to showcase your full catalog in a visual, browsable format.

Most podcast WordPress plugins are either bloated, outdated, or want to replace your entire workflow.

We just wanted a good-looking carousel and a player that follows you.

How it works:
1. Drop a shortcode on any page
2. Point it at your Apple Podcasts RSS feed
3. Episodes appear as a responsive, swipeable carousel
4. Click an episode — a floating player slides up from the bottom

The player stays fixed while visitors scroll.
They can listen and browse your site at the same time.

Two modes:
- RSS Feed mode — just paste your feed URL, episodes populate automatically
- Custom Post Type mode — full manual control over every episode

What's included:
- Responsive Swiper.js carousel (touch/swipe on mobile)
- Floating Apple Podcasts player
- RSS feed caching (1-hour transient)
- Custom taxonomy for episode categories
- Multiple carousels per page
- CSS custom properties for easy theming
- No fonts, no Bootstrap — blends with any theme

One shortcode:
[amplifi-pods feed="your-url" count="8"]

That's it.

Free, MIT licensed, and part of the amplifi.plugins suite.
