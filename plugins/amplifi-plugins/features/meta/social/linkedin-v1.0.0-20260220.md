We just open-sourced a WordPress plugin that generates SEO meta for your entire site with AI — for under $1.

It's called amplifi.meta.

Here's what it does:

Select 100 posts. Click generate. Get unique title tags, meta descriptions, and focus keyphrases for all of them in about a minute.

The cost? Roughly $0.002 per post.

That's 20 cents for 100 pages of fully optimized SEO meta.

But it goes further:

It also generates FAQ sections based on your content.
Deploys them with proper FAQPage schema so Google can show rich results.
And adds JSON-LD structured data (Organization + Article) automatically.

All integrated directly with Yoast SEO.

The problem it solves:

Most WordPress sites have dozens — sometimes hundreds — of posts with:
- No meta description
- No focus keyphrase
- No structured data
- No FAQ schema

Fixing this manually takes days.
Premium SEO plugins charge $50-600/year and still only generate meta one post at a time.

amplifi.meta does it in bulk, for pennies, in minutes.

What's included:
- Bulk meta editor with sortable post table
- AI-generated titles, descriptions, and keyphrases
- FAQ generation with accordion display
- FAQPage + Article + Organization JSON-LD
- Dark mode
- Works with any OpenAI model
- Free and MIT licensed

It's the second plugin in the amplifi.plugins suite.
